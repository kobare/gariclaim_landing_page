Everything you need to run your garage

From vehicle intake to release — job cards, parts, invoicing, payroll and full accounting in one place.

Job Cards & Repair Tracking

Open a job card for every vehicle, assign technicians, log labour and parts, and track progress from intake to completion.

Insurance Jobs

Record claim and policy numbers against each job card, along with insurer and assessor details. Keep everything the insurer needs on hand so the job moves smoothly.

Parts Procurement & LPOs

Issue LPOs to suppliers for parts and stock in bulk, track deliveries, and record supplier invoices. Parts flow into shared inventory, so any job card can draw from them without creating a separate order.

Vehicle & Customer Records

Track every vehicle by plate, VIN, make and model. Link repairs, parts, claims and invoices to the vehicle and its owner for full service history.

Invoicing & Quotations

Create quotations and estimates, convert approved ones into job cards, and raise invoices from completed work — including supplemented jobs.

POS / Over-the-Counter Sales

Sell spare parts, oil, and accessories at the counter. Instant receipts, thermal printing, and cash or mobile money payment records.

Spare Parts Inventory

Track stock across bins and branches. Auto-deduct parts consumed on job cards, and view full movement history.

QC, Inspection & Vehicle Release

Run quality checks before handover, capture before / after photos with a built-in viewer, collect a signed customer satisfaction note, and issue release authorization with a signed release letter.

Multi-Branch & Workspaces

Run multiple garage branches under one organization. Switch between workspaces for separate garages or business entities.

Team & Permissions

Invite front desk, technicians, store keepers and accountants with role-based access. Owners and admins control who sees what.

Chart of Accounts & VAT

Pre-loaded Kenyan chart of accounts tuned for garages. Auto-calculate VAT on parts, labour and insurance jobs. KRA PIN and tax registration management.

Audit Logs & Security

Every change is tracked with full audit history. Soft-deletes protect against accidental data loss. OTP verification for sensitive actions.

Purchases & Expenses

Record supplier bills, utilities, rent, and overheads. Track payables, payment terms, and get a clear view of your garage's spending.

Credit Notes & Refunds

Issue credit notes for customer invoices and supplier bills. Handle insurance adjustments, partial refunds, and corrections with a full audit trail.

Banking & Reconciliations

Record bank and M-Pesa transactions, then match and reconcile them against your statements. No API connections required.

Payroll, Contracts & Leave

Run payroll daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Manage permanent, contract and casual staff, track timesheets and leave, and auto-calculate PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, NITA.

Salary Advances & Loans

Manage salary advances and staff loans. Deduct repayments from payroll in installments as agreed, with full tracking of outstanding balances.

Accounting & Reports

Trial balance, balance sheets, general ledger, journal entries, fixed asset register, and adjusting entries — full double-entry accounting built in.
